How we work

A short, deliberate sequence.

01

Discover

Understand the problem, the audience, and the content before a single pixel moves.

02

Design

Systems first — type, motion, and layout rules that hold past the hero section.

03

Build

Production code, real performance budgets, accessibility from the start.

04

Refine

Ship, watch how people actually scroll, and tune against that.
